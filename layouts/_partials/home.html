HELLO, WE’RE STILLHET.

The world has enough noise.
Let’s find you a little quiet.

Some days, you don’t need another recommendation, another list, or another thing to keep up with. You just need somewhere to go.

We’re making a small app to help you find peaceful places in nature, close to home. One thoughtful suggestion, so you can stop searching and start being there.

QUIET PLACES SHOULD STAY THAT WAY.

A little care.
For you. And for the place.

Finding a peaceful place shouldn’t take its peace away. We’re building Stillhet around that simple thought.

  • Thoughtful suggestions that help spread the footsteps.
  • No social feed. No public visit history. Room for privacy.
