WHAT IS STILLHET?

Work is done.
Where can you go to unwind?

You’ve spent the day at a screen. You’d like a quiet walk in the woods or a little time by the water, but you don’t know where to go — and don’t feel like researching it.

That’s the moment Stillhet is being built for. Tell it what kind of nature you want and how far from home you’re willing to go. The aim is one considered area to explore, with practical details to help you decide and head outside.

Why I started Stillhet

THE STORY BEHIND STILLHET.

It started with silence.
Far from home.

Could a little of that peace
be closer to home?

I spend my working days writing software. But in Swedish Lapland, walking Kungsleden from Abisko with a tent, a sleeping bag and food on my back, I found something I keep looking for: silence. No cities, no signal. Just open landscape, above the Arctic Circle.

Coming home from Lapland, I wondered whether finding a little peace really needed a journey of thousands of kilometres. What about a place you could reach just after work?

That question became Stillhet. I’m exploring a small app that could help us find those places closer to home. It’s still taking shape, one idea and one experiment at a time.

QUIET PLACES SHOULD STAY THAT WAY.

A little care.
For you. And for the place.

Finding a peaceful place shouldn’t take its peace away. We’re building Stillhet around that simple thought.

The places we store will only be used to suggest areas to explore. Users will never be able to browse our database or search for individual spots in a list or directory.

  • Areas to explore, not secret spots to expose.
  • Thoughtful suggestions that help spread the footsteps.
  • No social feed. No public visit history. Room for privacy.

take a breath. leave only light footsteps.
